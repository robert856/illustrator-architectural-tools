Chain dimensions — furniture sections

  1. Choose Select > Deselect in Illustrator.
  2. Press A for the white arrow. Click an anchor at the bottom of the furniture.
  3. Hold Shift and click one anchor at each next height: base top, cabinet top, crown top. Click the actual anchor squares, not whole edges.
  4. Choose Vertical — right above (or left), then check your selection below. Three distinct heights give two dimensions. Anchors sharing a height are combined automatically.
