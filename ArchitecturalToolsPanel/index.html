Use the white arrow (A) and Shift-click anchors for Two points or Chain. Three distinct levels produce two chain dimensions. Use the circular arrow to check your selection.

Choose a tool, a side, then Create dimensions. Aligned measures the direct distance between two anchors; its up/left arrows choose side 1, down/right choose side 2.

Width and Height dimensions move with their grouped artwork. Point and chain dimensions are separate annotations: recreate them after moving source objects. Resizing does not update labels.

To reposition a point or chain segment, select that dimension inside its group and Apply offset. To reposition a width or height from the whole assembly, choose Width or Height first. Apply offset preserves the existing side and scale.
